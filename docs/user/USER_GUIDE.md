# F1 Market User Guide

Welcome to F1 Market! This guide will help you understand how to use the platform to trade shares in F1 drivers and teams.

## What is F1 Market?

F1 Market is a fantasy prediction market for Formula 1 racing. You can:
- **Buy shares** in drivers and teams before races
- **Sell shares** to lock in profits or cut losses
- **Earn payouts** when events settle based on race results

This is a **fantasy game using fictional credits**. No real money is involved.

## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

F1 Market uses passwordless login with email:

1. Click **Login** on the homepage
2. Enter your email address
3. Click **Send Code**
4. Check your email for a 6-digit code
5. Enter the code to log in

That's it! No password to remember. The code expires after 10 minutes.

### Your First Login

After logging in, you'll see your **Dashboard** with:
- Your wallet balance (starting credits)

## Understanding the Market

### What Are Shares?

Shares represent your "bet" on how a driver or team will perform.

- **Buy shares** = You think they'll do well
- **Sell shares** = You want to exit your position

When the race finishes, shares pay out based on actual performance.

### How Prices Work

Prices change based on supply and demand:
- **More buyers** = Price goes up
- **More sellers** = Price goes down

This is automatic - there's no waiting for someone else to take the other side of your trade. You can always buy or sell instantly.

### Market Status

Markets can be in different states:

| Status | What It Means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Open** | Trading is active. You can buy and sell. |
| **Closed** | Trading paused. Usually during the race. |
| **Settled** | Race finished. Payouts have been made. |

## Trading

### How to Buy Shares

1. Navigate to **Markets** from the menu
2. Select an event (e.g., "Monaco Grand Prix")
3. Choose a driver or team market
4. Enter the number of shares you want to buy
5. Review the estimated cost
6. Click **Buy**

**Tips:**
- The price per share increases as you buy more
- You can preview the cost before confirming
- Make sure you have enough balance

### How to Sell Shares

1. Go to **Portfolio** to see your positions
2. Click on a position you want to sell
3. Enter the number of shares to sell (or click "Sell All")
4. Review the estimated payout
5. Click **Sell**

**Tips:**
- You can sell part of your position
- Selling reduces the market price
- Your payout is credited instantly

### Understanding Order Previews

Before you trade, you'll see:

**For buying:**
- **Quantity:** Number of shares
- **Estimated Cost:** Total you'll pay
- **Price Per Share:** Average price

**For selling:**
- **Quantity:** Number of shares
- **Estimated Payout:** Total you'll receive
- **Price Per Share:** Average price

Actual execution may differ slightly due to price movement between preview and trade.

## Portfolio Management

### Viewing Your Positions

Go to **Portfolio** to see all your holdings:

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Market | The driver/team and event |
| Shares | How many shares you own |
| Entry Price | Average price you paid |
| Current Price | Current market price |
| Unrealized P&L | Profit/loss if you sold now |
| Realized P&L | Profit/loss from closed trades |

### Understanding P&L

**Unrealized P&L** (paper profits/losses):
- Calculated as: (Current Price - Entry Price) x Shares
- Changes as the market moves
- Only becomes real when you sell

**Realized P&L** (actual profits/losses):
- Recorded when you sell shares
- Also includes settlement payouts
- Final result of your trades

### Position Details

Click on any position to see:
- Full trading history
- Price chart
- Entry and exit details

## Settlement & Payouts

### What is Settlement?

Settlement is when an event finishes and positions are paid out based on actual results.

1. Race finishes
2. Results are recorded (positions, points)
3. Payout per share is calculated for each market
4. All positions receive their payout
5. Market status changes to "Settled"

### How Payouts are Calculated

Payouts are based on race performance:
- **Better performance** = Higher payout per share
- **Worse performance** = Lower payout per share

For example, if a driver wins:
- Their shares might pay out 1.0 credits each

If a driver finishes last:
- Their shares might pay out 0.1 credits each

The exact formula depends on the scoring rules for each sport.

### Receiving Your Payout

When settlement happens:
1. Your shares are converted to credits
2. Credits are added to your wallet
3. A "Settlement" entry appears in your ledger
4. Your position disappears (shares = 0)

You don't need to do anything - it's automatic!

## Reading Price Charts

Markets show price history charts:

- **X-axis:** Time
- **Y-axis:** Price

**What to look for:**
- **Upward trend:** More buying than selling
- **Downward trend:** More selling than buying
- **Spikes:** Large trades moved the price

Price history helps you understand market sentiment about a driver or team.

## Tips for Success

### Before You Trade

1. **Research** - Check standings, recent form, track history
2. **Compare prices** - Look at relative prices across markets
3. **Preview your trade** - Check the estimated cost/payout
4. **Consider timing** - Prices change as the race approaches

### Managing Risk

1. **Diversify** - Don't put all credits in one market
2. **Set limits** - Decide how much you're willing to lose
3. **Take profits** - Consider selling when you're up significantly
4. **Cut losses** - Consider selling if things go against you

### Common Mistakes

- **Buying at the top** - After a big rally, prices may fall
- **Ignoring fees** - Larger trades move prices more
- **Overtrading** - Too many trades can erode profits
- **Not checking balance** - Ensure you have enough credits

## FAQ

### Can I make real money?

No. F1 Market uses **fictional credits** only. There is no real money, withdrawals, or deposits. This is a fantasy game for entertainment.

### How do I get more credits?

New accounts start with credits. If you run out, contact support or wait for a credit reset (if available).

### Why did the price change before I could buy?

Prices update in real-time based on other users' trades. The price you see might change by the time you confirm. Use the preview feature to estimate costs.

### Can I cancel a trade?

No. Trades are executed instantly and cannot be cancelled. Preview your trade carefully before confirming.

### When do markets settle?

Markets typically settle shortly after the race finishes, once official results are confirmed. Settlement is automatic.

### Why is my payout less than the current price?

The current price is the *marginal* price for the next share. When you sell, you receive the average price for all your shares, which may be lower.

### How do I contact support?

Check the help section in the app or contact the administrators.
